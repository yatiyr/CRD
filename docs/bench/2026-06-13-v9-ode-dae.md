### Matrix-free Newton-Krylov (Cerid vs CVODE-SPGMR)

| target err | Cerid (ms/fev/GMRES-it) | CVODE-SPGMR (ms/fev/it) | Crush |
|---|---|---|---|

**Cerid wins at all matched-accuracy points (1.38–2.14×).** Root cause of initial loss: over-solved inner GMRES to fixed tol (1e-7); **fixed in-library** with inexact-Newton forcing (CVODE default 0.05). GMRES iters dropped 88–275 → 23–93.

## Honesty notes & moats

- **Determinism:** all methods run-twice bit-identical on a single thread. Parallel/Krylov determinism moats documented per slice.
- **Error-accuracy calibration:** Cerid controllers are well-tuned. Matched-accuracy comparison (both solvers achieve the same error) is the fair axis.
- **Stiff crusher:** BDF, RODAS4, Radau, and matrix-free Krylov all beat CVODE by 5×–9× at matched accuracy.
- **CVODES sensitivities:** Cerid's 5.57× gain came from a **fix to the in-library algorithm** (not just faster kernels) — the block-diagonal factorization economy. No parallel/special asterisks.
- **Follow-ups named, not silent:** TR-BDF2 at long horizon / tiny-n overhead / large-n MOL board row / native RK interpolants = all listed.

## Verdict

**v9 ODE/DAE cluster crushes SUNDIALS CVODE/ARKODE (5.9×–9.7× stiff · 1.14×–2.38× IMEX · 1.38×–2.14× matrix-free Krylov · 5.57× sensitivities) and Boost.odeint (1.6× nonstiff) with the determinism + allocation-free WCET moats that general ODE solvers structurally lack.**

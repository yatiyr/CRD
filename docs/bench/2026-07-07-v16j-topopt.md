# 2026-07-07 -- v16-j: adjoint topology optimization (SIMP) vs MATLAB top88

**What shipped (`topopt.hpp`):** density-based SIMP compliance minimization -- the applied face of the v16-g
implicit-differentiation lane. Minimise `c(ρ)=fᵀu` s.t. `K(ρ)u=f` (Q4 plane-stress FEA) + a volume constraint, with
the gradient by the DISCRETE ADJOINT. For self-adjoint compliance this is the classic SIMP sensitivity
`dc/dρ_e = −p·ρ_e^{p−1}(E0−Emin)·u_eᵀ·KE·u_e` -- differentiate the SOLUTION `K⁻¹f`, never the solver iterations (the
IFT of the linear solve). Self-contained: the top88 Q4 KE, a **banded-Cholesky direct solve** (the FEA K is symmetric
with half-bandwidth `2·nely+5` -- exact + condition-independent, immune to the SIMP void-element ill-conditioning that
cripples CG), a sensitivity filter, and an OC update.

## Gate (`test_topopt.cpp`, win-debug, 306 asserts)
- **The adjoint sensitivity passes the dolfin-adjoint-class TAYLOR-REMAINDER test:** `r(ε)=|c(ρ+εδ)−c(ρ)−ε·∇c·δ|`
  halves→quarters (2nd-order ratio ~4) => the gradient is EXACT; and central FD agrees to `<1e-5`.
- **The full OC optimisation** strictly reduces compliance, holds the volume constraint (`vol=0.500±0.02`), and is
  **bit-deterministic** run-to-run (exact `==` on the design + compliance).
- Full autodiff suite **3255 asserts / 116 cases** green.

## ★★ CRUSH vs MATLAB top88 (the reference)
**Config:** Cerid g++ 13.3 `-O3 -march=native`, WSL 1T `taskset -c 4`; **MATLAB R2026a** (Andreassen et al. 2011 88-line
top88, ft=1 sensitivity filter, sparse-direct `\`). Same canonical MBB beam, volfrac=0.5, penal=3, rmin=1.5.
Harnesses `external/crd_v16j_topopt_bench.cpp` + `build/crd_v16j_topopt_bench.sh` + `scripts/v16j_top88.m`.

Matched to ~5 significant figures (the residual is the OC bisection tolerance / path, not the FEA -- the Taylor test
proves Cerid's FEA + adjoint are exact). The physics matches the reference.

★ **SPEED:**

| mesh | **Cerid (banded direct)** | top88 (MATLAB) | **speedup** |
|--|--:|--:|--:|
| 60×20 | **99 ms** | 799 ms | **8.1×** |
| 120×40 | **1 944 ms** | 4 443 ms | **2.3×** |

The banded Cholesky (exact, condition-independent, cache-friendly contiguous band) beats MATLAB's general sparse
factorization + interpreter overhead. (A first cut used a matrix-free Jacobi-PCG solve -- correct + Taylor-gated but
2.7–7.5× SLOWER than top88 because the SIMP `Emin=1e-9` void elements make K severely ill-conditioned; swapping to the
banded direct solve was a 22× speedup and flipped the loss to an 8.1× win. Recorded because the CG loss was real
before the fix.)

★ **DETERMINISM:** the optimisation replays bit-for-bit run-to-run (exact `==`, gated). MATLAB/top88 with a
multi-threaded BLAS is not bit-reproducible.

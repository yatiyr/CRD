## What changed

  (fourteen change presets, five complete presets, one diagnostic), the eight owning jobs with their runners, and the
  four path classes (register, documentation, build-system, source) in match order.
- [scripts/check-ci-tiers.py](../../scripts/check-ci-tiers.py): the mapping validator, registered as the
  `crd-ci-tiers` CTest, a preflight step, a repository step and a tooling test.
- [scripts/ci-tier.py](../../scripts/ci-tier.py): `resolve` (event and path classification to tier, expected jobs
  and per-job preset lists as workflow outputs and a summary table) and `conclude` (the required aggregate).
- [scripts/ci-evidence.py](../../scripts/ci-evidence.py): `inventory` (`ctest --show-only=json-v1`) and `bundle`
  (`conclusion.json`, copied JUnit and inventory, job summary; never touches the exit code).
- [ci.yml](../../.github/workflows/ci.yml): a non-matrix `preflight` job (actionlint moved here, mapping check,
  base fetch, resolution); `repository` needs preflight; the eight heavy jobs need preflight, are gated on a non-empty
  list and take their matrices from the resolution; a new `windows-native` lane (`win-vs` on the VS 2026 image, Debug
  sandbox profile, repository and native-only guards under multi-config CTest); every test lane records its
  inventory, writes JUnit and ends in the two-sided register gate (previously Windows only); an always-run evidence
  bundle (recording `job.status`, so a failed configure or build is a failed lane, not a skipped test step) and an
  `evidence-<preset>` artifact on every lane; `schedule` (03:00 UTC), `workflow_dispatch` with a `tier` input; a
  concurrency group that cancels only superseded pull-request runs; the `required` aggregate. The advisor's close-out
  added Ninja to the native lane because the guard fixtures it runs configure with `-G Ninja`.
- `tests/CMakeLists.txt`: the `crd-ci-tiers` guard. `test-repository-tools.py`: eight new cases (mapping, failure
  modes, classification and resolution, real revisions, the required verdicts, evidence bundle, missing inputs, glob
  semantics).
- Docs: [design/ci-tiers.md](../design/ci-tiers.md); BUILDING (6,972 bytes) links the tiers and drops the

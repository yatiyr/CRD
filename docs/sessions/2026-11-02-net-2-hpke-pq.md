## What landed

- **The KEMs** (decision H2; draft-ietf-hpke-pq-05, draft-irtf-cfrg-concrete-hybrid-kems-04 and the CG framework of
  draft-irtf-cfrg-hybrid-kems-12): ML-KEM-768 and ML-KEM-1024 (0x0041, 0x0042) and MLKEM768-X25519 (X-Wing, 0x647A),
  MLKEM768-P256 (0x0050) and MLKEM1024-P384 (0x0051), named by the provider identifiers of their components
  (`MlKem768`, `X25519Mlkem768`, ...), as `X25519` names DHKEM(X25519). `HpkeSizes` gains `encapsulation` (Nenc).
- **DeriveKeyPair** is SHAKE256's LabeledDerive(ikm, "DeriveKeyPair", "", Nsk) under the KEM's suite_id
  (draft-ietf-hpke-hpke section 4.4; unchanged from revision 03 to 06). Its floor is 32 bytes for these KEMs, not
  Nsk: an MLS suite of HKDF-SHA384 derives node keys from 48-byte secrets, fewer than ML-KEM's 64-byte Nsk.
- **Hybrids**: the 32-byte seed is expanded by SHAKE256 into the ML-KEM seed and Group_T.Nseed bytes, the scalar the
  first Nscalar-byte chunk in [1, n - 1]; the shared secret is SHA3-256(ss_PQ || ss_T || ct_T || ek_T || label). An
  X25519 half's all-zero result is the secret RFC 7748 computes, as X-Wing specifies (refusing it would split a group
  whose other members follow the specification); a NIST-curve element or recipient half off the curve is refused.
- **Checks**: `hpke_check_public_key` (the agreement's check, FIPS 203's input check through the provider's new
  `check_kem_public_key`, the curve check of a hybrid's NIST-curve half), which MLS's leaf, init and node keys now
  pass instead of `check_agreement_public_key`.
- **The provider**: `sha3_256` and `shake256` (FIPS 202).
- **The register**: `crypto.hpke-pq`, `crypto.hybrid-kems`, `crypto.sha3` (implemented) and `mls.pq-ciphersuites`
  (planned, draft-ietf-mls-pq-ciphersuites-07 of 2026-11-01, in IESG processing; its eleven codepoints unverified until
  IANA lists them). The drafts were read in full and the HPKE codepoints checked against IANA's HPKE registry (updated
  2026-04-16) on 2026-11-02. `register-review.py plan` stopped on `tls.ech`'s TLS Alerts codepoint (a registry it had
  no source for); it now reads the TLS Alerts, HPKE (whose records name themselves in `kem`, `kdf` and `aead`
  elements) and MLS Cipher Suites registries, with a test, and a light review
  ([report](2026-11-02-standards-register-light-review.md)) was applied: 72 records read, two revisions updated
  (draft-ietf-plants-merkle-tree-certs-08, draft-ietf-quic-qlog-main-schema-15), the snapshot moved to 2026-11-02.
  The report's eleven proposed amendments are the post-quantum suites' codepoints, which IANA does not list yet;
  they are marked unverified, and nothing is to be corrected until IANA lists them.
- **The crypto inventory** gains the three implemented entries; its cerid-package classification record was
  restated (`gen-crypto-inventory.py --review cerid-package`).
- **The vectors**: the working group's `test-vectors.json` at the draft-ietf-hpke-pq-05 tag, pinned as
  `hpke-pq-vectors` (a testing download, SHA-256 and size in `cmake/pins.json`).
- **Fuzzing**: `crd-fuzz-crypto-mls`'s HPKE mode takes any suite of the profile (its eight KEMs, both KDFs, all three
  AEADs) instead of the four MLS suites, checks each derived public key with `hpke_check_public_key` and seeds a
  round trip and a changed one with every KEM (18 seeds).

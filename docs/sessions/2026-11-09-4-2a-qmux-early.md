## What landed

- `TlsSession::hold_tickets` and `remember_parameters`: a stream protocol's server parameters reach its tickets whether the ticket or the parameters came first (S9's record field,
  the provider's own reference while waiting). `remembered_parameters` and `send_half_rtt`.
- `QmuxConnection`: a resumed client sends its parameters and frames as early data under the ticket's limits, within the ticket's allowance; a server reads them marked replayable and
  answers at 0.5-RTT with its Finished still to come; a refusal puts the streams back and sends everything again, parameters first; accepted limits may not fall.
- `QuicStreamSet::on_all_acked` and `QuicDatagrams::hold_outcomes`: frames sent early count as delivered only once the server accepted them.
- `kQuicMaxEarlyContextBytes` now lives beside `kQuicEarlyParameterBytes`; the QMux fuzz targets share `qmux_fuzz_fixture.hpp`.

## Evidence

- `crd-net-tests` `[qmux]`, `[qmux-host]`, `[qmux-early]`: 78 cases on linux-gcc-debug, 110 with `[udp]`, `[poller]`, `[tcp]` on win-debug (`WSAPoll`). `crd-crypto-tests` `[early]`, 20 cases.
- 49 source mutations killed. They found a real defect: a client whose nothing-to-send turn was taken for a full allowance stopped sending early for good. One survivor is equivalent (holding tickets without
  a store: nothing arrives to hold), and one showed a redundant check, removed.

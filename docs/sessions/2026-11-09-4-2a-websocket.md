## What landed

- `websocket.hpp`: RFC 6455's frame headers, masking, Close payloads and opening handshake, sans-I/O, with RFC 9112's grammar around the head.
- `QmuxConnection` takes a `WebSocketConfig`: TLS agreeing on `http/1.1` turns the record path into an opening handshake and then one record per Binary message, no Size. No second connection or host; `ChannelCarrier` and `QmuxHost` did not change. One port serves QMux and WebSocket by ALPN.
- `crypto::legacy_sha1` for `Sec-WebSocket-Accept` only; `http_is_token`, `http_same_ignoring_case`, `http_target_valid` and `http_origin_allowed` are shared with HTTP/3 and WebTransport instead of copied.
- `websocket` is implemented in the standards register. draft-ietf-quic-qmux-02 has no WebSocket binding (read 2026-11-09), so the mapping is recorded as the crate's and proven against it.
- `QmuxHost::read_left` (found by the interop): a write that found the peer gone left what the peer had sent unread, so a peer that reset behind its last bytes read as a plain Reset; what the socket holds is now read first, with a bounded count of reads.
- Also fixed: two `id="channels"` anchors in network-foundation.md (S60 is now `channel-link`).

## Evidence

- `crd-net-tests` `[websocket]`, 40 cases: linux-gcc-debug, linux-gcc-asan, linux-clang-tsan, linux-gcc-release (LTO) and win-debug (MSVC) pass; the whole `[net]` tag (552 cases) passes on linux-gcc-debug. Tidy clean on every touched file.
- `crd-net-qmux-interop-crate` gains 36 WebSocket cases, Cerid against the `qmux` crate's WebSocket transport over tokio-tungstenite 0.30 and rustls, both ways round: closes with code and reason, transfers past every window, MAX_STREAMS turning over, uni streams, resets and stops, datagrams at the limit, QX_PING and the RTT, relays cutting the bytes into pieces of 1 to 7, a silent path, and a refused subprotocol (400). The crate's stop and uni cases hold a second before closing, as its reset case does: a CONNECTION_CLOSE ends its readers and abandons a write the WebSocket has not finished.
- `crd-fuzz-net-websocket` (new): 407,081 executions in 277 s on linux-clang-fuzz, corpus 3 to 3285, no artifact. Parsers alone, then a server and a client behind a real TLS peer.
- 50 source mutations: 48 fail the tests and pass once restored, across key validity, reserved bits, the mask side, control frames, minimal lengths, the 1009 limit, Close codes and UTF-8, Origin, path, bodies, the head search, Accept, subprotocol rules, a bare CR, the field limit, masking itself and its randomness, Close echo and statuses, refusals, frames behind the head, ALPN dispatch, 0-RTT and `read_left`. The other two: one is equivalent (skipping the in-place path for an unfragmented record changes only speed), one showed a dead guard in `established()`, removed.

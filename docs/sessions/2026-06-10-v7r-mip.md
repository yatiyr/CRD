## ⭐⭐ The oracle scan caught a real bug (fixed in-session, no debt)

Instance 20 of the scan: B&B returned −1.4354 with a clean "Solved" proof while exhaustive enumeration said
−1.5096 — and the tree closed after only TWO nodes. The diagnostic sequence: (1) a root-relaxation
cross-check (simplex vs the v7-k IPM) EXONERATED the simplex — both gave −2.1974 to 1e-9 — pinning the bug
to the B&B itself; (2) the trace: `lo`/`up` are pointers INTO `node_lo`/`node_up`, and the first
`push_node` (push_back) can REALLOCATE those arrays — the second child's bound restoration
`up_child[branch] = up[branch]` then read freed memory, giving that child a garbage (empty) box that was
"pruned by infeasibility", deleting the subtree containing the true optimum. **Fix:** snapshot the parent
bound before any push (commented at the site). The root cross-check stays in the test as a permanent gate.
ASan re-run green after the fix (the read was exactly its class of catch).

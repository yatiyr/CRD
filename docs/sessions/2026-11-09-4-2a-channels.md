## What landed

- `ChannelLink` and `ChannelCarrier` (`channel_link.hpp`): the one reader and writer of NET.3's frames, for QUIC, QMux, the raw UDP path and a WebTransport session. Until now nothing in the engine called `StreamAssembler`, `DatagramAssembler` or `Admission`.
- A reliable ordered channel is one stream, an unordered one a stream per write, an unreliable one datagrams. A channel's priority is its stream's urgency.
- In-band padding on WebTransport only, never cutting a frame. Readers held by `Wait`, woken by the rate gate or a freed place. A refused stream stops alone; broken framing fails the link once.
- `WebTransport::set_urgency`, `send_room`, `max_datagram`, `can_open`; `Http3Connection::max_datagram`; `QuicStreamSet::can_open`; `StreamAssembler::between_frames` and `refused`.

## What landed

- `bench_vm.py create --once HOURS`: a spot machine that stays running and deletes itself with its disk at the deadline or on a
  preemption, so a lost session bills nothing. Test in `crd-ci-platform`.
- [Board](../bench/2026-11-07-hesap-net-avx512.md) on a Xeon Platinum 8581C: one machine, 21 minutes, about 0.06 USD, deleted in the
  same batch; only the stopped launcher remains in the project.
- The board found the bounded float's AVX-512 encode 1.65x behind its own AVX2 body. `code_of` now clamps with `min` and `max`, a
  local copy of the format keeps its broadcasts out of the loop, and the decode's unsigned check is one compare past a sign flip.
  Bounded encode 0.61 to 0.23 ns (AVX2 0.37 to 0.24), decode 0.25 to 0.21, smallest three's encode 2.66 to 2.02.

## Evidence

After the change, on linux-gcc-release: `crd-hesap-net-tests [quantize]` and `crd-hesap-quantize-peer-tests` pass at the native,
avx512, avx2, sse2 and portable levels, bytes equal to serialize and networked-physics. NaN and -0 cases are in `test_quantize.cpp`.

## What the run did not settle

- RaptorQ's planned rows at K = 10 (0.79x nanorq, which also runs AVX-512 GFNI here) and K = 101 (0.96x). Not line splits: 64-byte
  symbols moved nothing, and that experiment was reverted. The profile had too few samples to name the phase.
- A slip: the raw board files were not copied before the delete (the fetch failed on a `~` path and the commands were chained), so
  the board's figures are the output read during the run, one run per table.

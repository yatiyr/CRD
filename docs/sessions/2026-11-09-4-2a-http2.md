## What landed

- `http2.hpp`: `Http2Connection` (RFC 9113), sans-I/O over a byte stream, on `HpackDecoder`/`HpackEncoder` and the message checks HTTP/3 uses. Frames read where they lie, one field block gathered at a time.
- Every frame type with its refusals; stream states with a ring of the last closures; both flow-control windows with padding and retroactive SETTINGS; 431 for a section over the limit; GOAWAY both ways; extended CONNECT (RFC 8441 section 3).
- Flood limits on `RateGate`: rapid reset, provoked resets, SETTINGS, PING, PRIORITY, empty frames, tiny WINDOW_UPDATEs, CONTINUATION bounded by the block; reading stops when the output has no room for an answer.
- `crd-fuzz-net-http2`. `http.h2` stays `planned` in the register until interop.

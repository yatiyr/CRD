## What landed

- `http3.hpp`: `Http3Connection` over `QuicStreamSet` and `QuicDatagrams`: control and QPACK streams, SETTINGS, every frame on every stream, request and response state machines, interim responses, trailers, CONNECT, Extended CONNECT (RFC 9220), HTTP datagrams (RFC 9297 section 2), GOAWAY both ways, 0-RTT's remembered settings, grease. Push is never offered.
- `http_message.hpp`: what makes a message well formed on either framed version: the HTTP/2 path's single owner of those rules.
- `http_capsule.hpp`: RFC 9297 section 3's capsules, reading from a stream split anywhere without gathering a value, for any HTTP data stream.
- `QuicStreamSet`: `slot_of` (per-stream state beside the QUIC slot), `copy_readable` (bytes across the ring's wrap, unconsumed), `send_room`, `consume(..., keep_end)` (the end stays observable until the layer has acted on it) and the slot in `QuicStreamEvent`.
- `crd-fuzz-net-http3` and its corpus. RFC 9297 read in full: `http.datagrams` verified. Its register state and `http.h3` stay `planned` until interop.

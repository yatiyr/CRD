## What landed (capability `network.transport`)

- `QuicConnection`: a resumed client sends streams and datagrams in 0-RTT packets of the original version under its
  ticket's remembered parameters; acceptance with a lowered limit closes with PROTOCOL_VIOLATION; rejection drops the
  0-RTT recovery state, resets the streams and reports queued datagrams Lost. A server names its early data context
  (`write_quic_early_parameters` plus the application's `early_context`), discards the Early level when it did not
  accept and its 0-RTT keys three PTOs after the first 1-RTT packet. A bounded ring (`held_packets`) holds packets
  whose keys may still come and reads them once the keys come.
- `QuicStreamSet`: `set_peer_parameters()` only raises (open streams too), `reset_all()`, and
  `QuicStreamRead::early_bytes` marking replayable bytes; `QuicDatagrams`: `oldest_early()`, `reject_early()`;
  `QuicRecovery::reject_early()`; `QuicPacketProtection::keys_pending()`.
- Fixed on the way: a Retry would strand 0-RTT stream frames (they now go again); a 0-RTT packet that did not fit
  behind a full Initial left a client datagram under 1200 bytes (the earlier packet now pads).

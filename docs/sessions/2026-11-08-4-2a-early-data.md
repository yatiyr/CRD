## What landed (capability `network.transport`)

- `QuicConnection`: a resumed client sends streams and datagrams in 0-RTT packets of the original version under its
  ticket's remembered parameters; acceptance with a lowered limit closes with PROTOCOL_VIOLATION; rejection drops the
  0-RTT recovery state, resets the streams and reports queued datagrams Lost. A server names its early data context
  (`write_quic_early_parameters` plus the application's `early_context`), discards the Early level when it did not
  accept and its 0-RTT keys three PTOs after the first 1-RTT packet. A bounded ring (`held_packets`) holds packets
  whose keys may still come and reads them once the keys come.
- `QuicStreamSet`: `set_peer_parameters()` only raises (open streams too), `reset_all()`, and
  `QuicStreamRead::early_bytes` marking replayable bytes; `QuicDatagrams`: `oldest_early()`, `reject_early()`;
  `QuicRecovery::reject_early()`; `QuicPacketProtection::keys_pending()`.
- Fixed on the way: a Retry would strand discarded 0-RTT stream frames (they now go again), and a 0-RTT packet that
  did not fit behind a full Initial left the client's datagram under 1200 bytes (the earlier packet now pads).

## Evidence

- linux-gcc-asan: crd-net-tests pass (313 cases, 16.5 million assertions), the 10 `[quic-early]` cases among them.
- 17 source mutations, all caught: rejection ignored, streams or queued datagrams kept on it, the 0-RTT recovery
  state kept, a rejecting server keeping its keys, no 0-RTT key timer, nothing held, held packets never read, a
  Retry stranding 0-RTT data, no 0-RTT sent, remembered parameters unused, open streams keeping their remembered
  credit, stream or datagram marks lost, a lowered limit passing, a context field left out, the Initial floor left
  to a packet that cannot fit.
- `crd-fuzz-net-quic-connection`: a 0-RTT bit (byte 0, bit 4) and three seeds; the corpus replays under ASan.
  libFuzzer first found the harness waiting 10 ms steps for 0-RTT data lost behind a PTO of minutes (it now moves to
  the next timer). A later run (13,570 executions, corpus 12 to 938) reported one UBSan object-size message on a
  QuicStreamRecord constructor that its input does not reproduce, alone, with the whole working corpus in one
  process or under the run's libFuzzer seed; a further 300 s (42,836 executions) found nothing. The input is in the
  committed corpus, so every lane's replay keeps it.
- Not yet run: the MSVC, clang-cl and ARM64 lanes (CI), and 0-RTT against a pinned peer implementation.

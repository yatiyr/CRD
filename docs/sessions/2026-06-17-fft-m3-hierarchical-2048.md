## TL;DR — M3 wins. Accelerating the 2048 sub-FFT too lifts 8M ~1.13× (→ ~0.94× MKL), exactly as predicted.

- **2048 sub-FFT (isolated): Choice B (64×32) = 1.260× over `execute_batched(2048,32)`, machine-eps** (Choice A
  32×64 = 1.224×; B chosen). Both clear the ≥1.10× phase gate. Leaves survive their regimes (codelet32@b2048 1.26×,
  codelet64@b1024 1.31×).
- **Wired into the gate alongside M2's 4096 path.** Now at 8M BOTH four-step sub-FFTs are hierarchical (n1=4096 +
  n2=2048); at 4M both are 2048; 16M unchanged (both 4096).
- **Full-FFT — three independent measurements agree** (unlike M2's wobbly 8M):
  - **Primary (clean): `m3_full` interleaved BASE/M3, same process, ~2–3% MKL drift/pair** — **8M 1.13–1.17× · 4M
    1.13–1.17× · 16M 1.06–1.08× engine speedup.**
  - **Corroboration: canonical `bench_fft_vs_refs`** (all-BASE-then-all-M3; ~14–22% thermal drift BETWEEN halves ⇒
    its cross-run engine ratio is corroboration, its **within-run ×MKL is the dashboard number**): 8M 1.115× /
    **~0.94× MKL**; 4M 1.239× / **~0.95–0.99× MKL** (range — 4M MKL swung 14% cross-run, don't harden to 0.99).
  - **8M ~0.94× MKL** matches the prompt's "≥1.10× ⇒ 8M ~0.92–0.94× MKL". Isolated 2048 sub-FFT = **1.26×**.
  - All machine-eps (1.1–1.2e-15). (An `m3_full` ×MKL of ~1.04 is NOT reported — it used a non-canonical harness
    that flatters Cerid; the canonical ~0.94 is the honest dashboard number.)
- **Decision:** M3 is a clean win and a **default-enable candidate** (pure win 2M/4M/8M/16M, machine-eps,
  deterministic, zero regression). Gate stays OFF in this change-set; **enabling requires the 18-config DoD (M4)**.

## Decomposition (Choice B, 2048 = 64×32; the M2 generalization)
N = N1·N2, element-major `scratch[n·BB+bb]`, n=N2·n1+n2, k=N1·k2+k1. For 2048: **N1=64, N2=32, BB=32**.
- Stage-1 FUSED = `codelet64_stage1_fused_64x32(scr, bbuf, b=N2·BB=1024, twr, twi)` (leaf codelet64, twiddle
  `W_2048^{n2·k1}`, transposed store to `bbuf[n2·(N1·BB=2048) + k1·32 + bb]`).
- Stage-2 PLAIN = `codelet32_batched(bbuf, out, b=N1·BB=2048)` — natural output. 2 passes (M2 Variant B form).
Choice A (32×64) was the other variant (gen-32 fused stage-1 → gen-64 stage-2); measured 1.224× < B's 1.260×.

## Why this lifts 8M (and 4M): the accelerated fraction
At 8M, M2 accelerated only n1=4096 (27%) ⇒ ~1.04×. M3 adds n2=2048 (the other ~24%) ⇒ ~51% accelerated ⇒ ~1.13×,
the same class as 16M. The gain scaling with the accelerated fraction (the M2 diagnosis) is confirmed: 8M went
1.04×→1.13× when the second sub-FFT became hierarchical. 4M (both sub-FFTs 2048) jumps to 1.24× (the biggest gain).

### 16M is unchanged BY CONSTRUCTION (not just "no regression")
M3 does not touch the 16M path: verified the regenerated `codelet64_stage1_fused_64x64` body is **byte-identical**
to M2's `codelet64_stage1_fused` (779 lines, empty diff — the generator is seed-fixed), and the ctor twiddle for
n=4096 is unchanged (`th=2π·n2·k1/4096`, index n2·64+k1, n2,k1∈[0,64)). So 16M's 1.06–1.08× here vs M2's
1.06–1.12× is the ±3% noise floor, not a real change.

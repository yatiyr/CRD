## What landed

- `StreamSocket`, `TcpSocket` and `TcpListener`; UDP and TCP share `native_socket.hpp`; `SocketError` gains Refused, Reset and
  TimedOut.
- `QmuxConnection`: QUIC's `QuicStreamSet` and `QuicDatagrams` over QMux records inside TLS 1.3, sans-I/O. This path has no stream or
  flow-control code of its own. `quic.qmux` is in the register with its four provisional codepoints generated;
  `QuicStreamLimits::in_order` carries QMux's ordering rule.
- A gap found on the way: `QuicConnection` passed a padding block of zero to every packet, so NET.3's `padded_size()` never applied on
  QUIC. `QuicConnectionConfig::padding_block` now reaches the packet writer.
- The draft's silences are decided in S52: a record of no frames, repeated STREAM data, tickets per path.

## Evidence

- `crd-net-tests` `[qmux]`, `[tcp]`, `[udp]`, `[quic-connection]`: 102 cases on linux-gcc-debug; `[qmux]`, `[tcp]` and `[udp]` on
  win-debug too.
- 29 source mutations killed (two survivors found the idle timer's edges, which now have tests).
- `crd-fuzz-net-qmux`: three libFuzzer runs on linux-clang-fuzz, 131,000 executions. The first stopped at 19,864 on a wild
  `memset` inside AWS-LC with a pointer as its length; its input replays clean 60 times of 60 and the later 111,000 executions
  found nothing. That is this host's known fault signature ([lesson](../../MEMORY.md)), not a Cerid defect shown; hosted fuzzing is the
  check: `net-qmux` legs of Host check run [42543967150](https://github.com/yatiyr/CRD/actions/runs/42543967150).
- One unreproduced failure on win-debug: a run of `[qmux],[tcp]` right after a rebuild showed one failed assertion, its output cut by
  `tail`; the assertion count points at `a TCP write past the host's buffers`. 945 later runs, 720 of them six at a time, passed. Hosted
  Windows lanes are the check, see Next.
